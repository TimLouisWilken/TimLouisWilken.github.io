Saudi Vision 2030: Can Renewables Reach 50 %?

Is Saudi Arabia's target of a 50 % renewable share in electricity by 2030 achievable?

2026

2026

Monte CarloEconometricsEnergy PolicyPython
Code

The analysis projects 2030 electricity demand with an econometric model and simulates the expansion of grid integration capacity and of PV and wind capacity as stochastic paths. Combining them gives a distribution of the renewable share in 2030.

Highlights

  • Simulated 2030 demand: median about 567 TWh, 90 % range about 527 to 609 TWh.
  • Renewable share in 2030 of only 31.0 to 35.8 %.
  • Even with 40 % annual tender growth and wind prioritised, the 50 % target is missed because of physical lead times and grid bottlenecks.
