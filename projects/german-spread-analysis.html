German Day-Ahead Spread Analysis

Is the daily price spread, the core of battery arbitrage, still tied to the price level? A reconstruction of FlexPower's blog analysis, extended to October 2026.

Until 2023 the German day-ahead price level and its daily high-low spread moved together, because gas set both. Since 2024 they have come apart: solar pushes midday prices down while the evening ramp is still set by gas. The analysis rebuilds the chart with Energy-Charts/SMARD data from 2018 to October 2026 and splits it by season.

Highlights

  • In winter the spread is still about 0.8 times the price level, as before 2023.
  • In spring and summer it has been 1.6 to 1.8 times the price level since 2024.
  • The annual mean spread rose from 111 (2024) to 124 (2025) and 159 EUR/MWh (2026 to date), while the price level rose only moderately.
  • The drop after June 2024 was seasonal; the rise of summer spreads from year to year is structural.
