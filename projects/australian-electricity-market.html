Australian Electricity-Market Dynamics

Battery storage and electricity price convergence: a threshold vector error-correction analysis of the South Australian and Victorian market, 2023–2025.

2026

2026

EconometricsTime Seriesstatsmodels
Code

South Australia runs on about 90 % wind and solar, and its battery fleet grew 4.7-fold in three years. The question is causal: do batteries measurably dampen wholesale prices, and does the effect depend on the price regime?

The hard part is simultaneity. Batteries discharge because prices are already spiking, so plain OLS even shows a positive effect on prices. Six identification strategies, from lagged regressors to 2SLS with a commissioning instrument, all point the same way once this is addressed: batteries prevent spikes from forming rather than blunting them once they have started.

Highlights

  • 52,608 half-hourly observations, January 2023 to December 2025.
  • Johansen cointegration and a Hansen–Seo threshold test select a three-regime TVECM (p = 0.030).
  • Estimated consumer welfare gain of 10.8 million AUD per year in the counterfactual.
  • SA–VIC price correlation rose from 0.38 (2023) to 0.80 (2025); the spike regime fell from 9.4 % to 5.0 % of intervals.
