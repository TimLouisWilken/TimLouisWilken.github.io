BESS Trading Simulator

An interactive simulator that walks through the decisions a battery trader makes: when to charge, discharge or wait across the GB markets.

2026

2026

Open the simulator →Code

We built the simulator, a fellow student and I, to understand how GB battery trading works in practice. It is not a market replica, but the reasoning behind each trade is the same: read the price signals, manage the state of charge, schedule and review.

Highlights

  • Live Elexon BMRS data: day-ahead prices, system prices, NIV and demand, wind and solar forecasts.
  • Day-ahead auction with a 48-period schedule builder, gate closure at 09:20 UK time.
  • Intraday re-optimisation as forecasts update, and imbalance trading with NIV-based cash-out.
  • Balancing-mechanism acceptances with merit-based probabilities and a P&L dashboard.
  • Guided lessons from arbitrage to market context, and a decision coach that adapts to price, battery state and forecast.
